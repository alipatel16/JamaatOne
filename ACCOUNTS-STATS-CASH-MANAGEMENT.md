# Accounts Stats and Cash Management

## Stats
- Default manager view is Today.
- Supports Today, This Month, and Custom Date Range.
- Shows received money, expenses, balance, payment income, other credits, and date-wise summaries.

## Cash management
- Cash received = CASH member payments + CASH day-book credit entries.
- Bank deposits are recorded separately against one of five configured bank accounts.
- Pending cash = total cash received - total deposited to banks.
- Partial deposits across multiple banks are supported.

## Permissions
- Admin and committee members can add and edit payment entries.
- Only ADMIN can delete a payment. This restriction exists both in the screen and mock API.
